# My Account Future Modules

Status: documented for launch, hidden in the live My Account navigation.

The following My Account modules exist as placeholders in `account.html`, but are intentionally hidden until the features are fully designed and implemented:

- `Sessions`: future session/device management, such as viewing active sessions and signing out other devices.
- `Contributor`: future contributor profile/tools for members who help improve listings, reports, or verification.
- `Admin`: future admin-only account area. This must stay hidden from normal members unless server-authorized admin checks are in place.

Launch rule:

- Do not show placeholder account modules in the user navigation.
- Do not expose admin controls from frontend-only checks.
- Re-enable each module only when the backend permissions, UX, and documentation are complete.
