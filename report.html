Report a Listing

Help us keep World Cigar Locator accurate and trustworthy.

Businesses may change policies, stop selling cigars, relocate, or update their smoking rules.

If something is incorrect or misleading, please let us know.

What Can Be Reported?

  • A location that no longer sells cigars
  • A lounge that no longer allows smoking
  • Incorrect address or contact information
  • Duplicate listings
  • Permanently closed businesses
  • Misleading or outdated information

How Review Works

All reports are reviewed manually.

We may update, temporarily remove, or permanently remove listings after review.

We do not make automated changes based solely on submissions.

Submit a Report

Please email us at:

reportlisting@worldcigarlocator.com

Include:

  • Store name
  • City / Country
  • What is incorrect
  • Any helpful details

Your email will only be used for follow-up regarding this report.
