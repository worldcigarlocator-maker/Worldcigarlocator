About World Cigar Locator

Welcome to the Global Humidor

World Cigar Locator exists for one simple reason:

If you are somewhere unfamiliar and want a cigar — you should instantly know where to go.

We help you discover small cigar shops, exclusive lounges, hidden gems, verified stores, cigar bars, and unexpected places that actually sell cigars — anywhere in the world.

Our goal is simple: To show you where you can buy a cigar and where you can comfortably enjoy one, wherever you may be traveling.

How We Define a Store

For us, a store is any place where you can buy a cigar.

It does not matter if cigars are their primary business. It does not matter if it is only a small humidor in the corner.

If you can purchase a cigar there — it qualifies.

  • Dedicated cigar shops
  • Hotels
  • Cafés
  • Whisky bars
  • Barber shops
  • Remote local stores

Availability is what matters.

How We Define a Lounge

A lounge is any place where you are allowed to sit and smoke and where there is at least a proper place to do so.

  • Members-only clubs
  • Luxury cigar lounges
  • Modest seating areas in small shops

If you can sit and enjoy your cigar there — we label it a lounge.

Built for Real-World Use

World Cigar Locator is not a lifestyle magazine. It is not a brand directory.

It is a practical tool.

You are in a new city. You want a cigar. You open WCL. You find one 10 minutes away — even if it is just a small café that quietly keeps a few good cigars behind the counter.

That is the promise.

Transparency & Accuracy

World Cigar Locator is built from public sources, location data (including Google Maps), manual research, and community feedback.

We have not personally visited every location worldwide.

Businesses may change ownership, policies, or product availability. Information may occasionally be outdated or incomplete.

If you notice something incorrect or misleading, please use the Report a Listing page so we can review it.

Every report is reviewed manually.
