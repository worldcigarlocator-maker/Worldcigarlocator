Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

This English version is the authoritative privacy text for WCL. Any translated versions are provided for convenience only.

World Cigar Locator (“WCL”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting personal data in accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and other applicable privacy rules.

This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Data Controller

World Cigar Locator is the data controller for personal data processed through this website.

For privacy-related inquiries, contact: info@worldcigarlocator.com.

2. What Data We Collect

a) Information You Provide

We may collect:

  • Email address
  • Display name or alias
  • Account settings and language preferences
  • Comments, ratings, favorites, and reports submitted through WCL
  • Listing suggestions and optional messages
  • Support or legal inquiries sent to WCL

Providing this information is voluntary, but some features require an account.

b) Automatically Collected Information

When you use the site, we may collect:

  • IP address
  • Browser and device information
  • Approximate location signals used for security or analytics
  • Pages visited and searches performed
  • Listing views, opens, website clicks, and report activity
  • Session identifiers, cookie consent state, and security signals

This data is used for security, analytics, abuse prevention, and platform improvement.

3. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

We process personal data under one or more of the following legal bases:

  • Contract or requested service, such as account access and user features
  • Legitimate interest, such as operating, securing, and improving WCL
  • Consent, where required for cookies, analytics, or optional features
  • Compliance with legal obligations

4. How We Use Personal Data

We use data to:

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Show favorites, ratings, comments, language settings, and account data
  • Review listing reports and listing submissions
  • Moderate comments and prevent misuse
  • Send account, listing, report, or support emails
  • Improve functionality, search, analytics, and performance
  • Monitor and prevent abuse, spam, fraud, or automated account creation

We do not sell personal data.

5. Data Processing & Infrastructure

To operate WCL, we use trusted third-party providers for hosting, security, database infrastructure, authentication, maps, email delivery, analytics, bot protection, and moderation support.

These may include providers such as Supabase, Cloudflare, Google Maps, Resend, GitHub Pages, and OpenAI-powered moderation tools. These providers process data on our behalf or provide services necessary for the platform.

We aim to use appropriate safeguards and access controls for these services.

6. Comments, Reports, and Moderation

Comments, listing reports, and listing suggestions may be reviewed manually and/or by automated tools to detect spam, abuse, unlawful content, off-platform sales, scams, or policy violations.

We may retain moderation logs and report history for operational, audit, legal, and quality control purposes.

7. Cookies and Analytics

WCL uses essential cookies and local storage required for login, account access, age confirmation, language settings, cookie consent, security, and core site functionality.

WCL may use privacy-safe aggregate analytics to understand listing views, store opens, website clicks, and general platform performance without storing analytics visitor IDs, session IDs, account IDs, email addresses, user geography, or IP-derived user location.

Optional enhanced analytics may be used to understand sessions, journeys, search patterns, map usage, and user geography. Where required by law, enhanced analytics should be based on consent and can be accepted or rejected through WCL cookie settings.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Blocking essential cookies may prevent parts of WCL from working correctly.

8. Data Retention

Personal data is retained only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, account management, safety, legal compliance, dispute handling, or operational quality control.

Account data may be retained while your account is active. Comments, reports, listing submissions, moderation records, and support messages may be retained for operational and audit purposes unless deletion is required by applicable law.

9. International Data Transfers

WCL is available internationally and uses cloud service providers that may process data outside your country or outside the European Economic Area (EEA). Where required, appropriate safeguards should be used in accordance with GDPR.

10. Your Rights Under GDPR

If you are located in the EU/EEA, you may have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction
  • Request deletion (“right to be forgotten”)
  • Restrict processing
  • Object to processing
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
  • Request data portability
  • Lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority

To exercise your rights, contact: info@worldcigarlocator.com.

11. Security

We use modern cloud infrastructure, access controls, bot protection, and database services to help protect personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction.

However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

12. Children and Minors

WCL is intended for adults of legal smoking age only. We do not knowingly create accounts for, or target, minors.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the site constitutes acceptance of any updates.
